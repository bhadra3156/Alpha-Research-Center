# AlphaResearch Scoring Rubric

## CHECK 1: FUNDAMENTALS (Must PASS)
- Revenue Growth: Positive 3yr CAGR + recent acceleration
- FCF Margin: >10% and positive trend
- Balance Sheet: Debt/Equity <1.0 and Current Ratio >1.5
- ROIC vs WACC: Positive spread
- Valuation: PEG <1.5, FCF Yield >4%

## CHECK 2: TECHNICAL PHASE (Must PASS)
- Stage 1 (Accumulation) or Stage 2 (Markup) = PASS
- 50-day MA > 200-day MA = Bullish
- Volume expanding on up days

## CHECK 3: SMART MONEY (At least ONE)
- Hedge Funds: Recent 13F increase
- Politicians: Recent buys (last 90 days)
- Insiders: Open-market Form 4 "P" buys
